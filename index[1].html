About Curious Clinics

Making early science and health education welcoming, memorable, and fun.

Curious Clinics is a free, community-focused educational program created by three high school students who believe that children should have opportunities to explore science and health topics from an early age.

Each week, we introduce a new topic from the basics of biology and mental health, while emphasizing why understanding our bodies, brains, and well-being matters. Sessions are designed to be interactive rather than lecture-based, using hands-on activities, presentations, demonstrations, and other engaging ways to learn.

Sessions typically last 1–2 hours. Locations vary depending on the session, and all programming is offered completely free of charge.

What can families expect?

Friendly instructors, age-appropriate explanations, and plenty of opportunities for students to ask questions and participate.
